Deployed app

Duits: receipt-to-expense tracker

Logging a purchase usually means typing it into a spreadsheet and remembering it later. Duits takes a receipt photo, a bank screenshot or one line of text, extracts the amount, merchant and items with Claude, and stores it in Supabase behind per-user auth. It ships as an iPhone-first PWA with budgets, recurring bills and month insights, and is live at duits-money-tracking.onrender.com.

Final year project

Breast cancer detection from histology images

My final-year project classifies BreaKHis histology slides as benign or malignant. I trained an EfficientNetV2-S backbone with a CBAM attention module on a patient-level split, then served it through FastAPI with Grad-CAM overlays that show which tissue regions drove each prediction. On the held-out test set it reaches 96.5% malignant recall and 0.874 AUC at a calibrated 0.51 threshold, with a Streamlit demo for single and batch analysis.

  • EfficientNetV2-S
  • CBAM
  • Grad-CAM
  • FastAPI
  • Streamlit
